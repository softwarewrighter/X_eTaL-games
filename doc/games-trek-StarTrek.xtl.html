librarygames/trek/StarTrek.xtl

Star Trek's rules, a library: trek.xtl (scripted), play.xtl (at the terminal) and the web page all use these. The classic Star Trek game as the COR24 BASIC demo plays it: an 8 by 8 galaxy of quadrants, each an 8 by 8 grid of sectors; find and destroy every Klingon before the stardate runs out, with warp and impulse drive, phasers, photon torpedoes and shields.

The game is one vector: 1 energy, 2 torpedoes, 3 shields, 4 stardate, 5 deadline, 6 and 7 the quadrant (row, column, 0 to 7), 8 and 9 the sector, 10 the status (0 playing, 1 won, 2 out of time, 3 ship lost, 4 resigned); then the galaxy as three planes of 64 (Klingons, bases, stars per quadrant) and the current quadrant's 64 sectors (0 empty, 1 the Enterprise, 2 a star, 3 a base, 4 and more a Klingon: its hit points plus 4).
