librarygames/trek-adventure/TrekAdventure.xtl

Trek adventure's rules, a library: trek-adventure.xtl (a scripted walkthrough), play.xtl (at the terminal) and the web page all use these. "Star Trek: Decaying Orbit": you wake alone on the Enterprise's bridge, its orbit decaying; find the toolkit and the engine coupler, prep the relay in engineering, install the coupler before the turns run out, and deal with the Klingon boarders on the way.

The game is tables, not branches: every line of text is one row of a table, with a key (a room, an event, an ending) and a condition on the state; a room's description or a message is the rows whose key it is and whose condition holds, picked by one mask. The rooms' exits are a 9 by 9 table, the items' places a vector.

The state is one vector: 1 room, 2 turns left, items held 3 phaser, 4 ID card, 5 toolkit, 6 coupler, 7 tribble (1 = carried), 8 relay prepped, 9 boarders aboard, 10 won, 11 dead, 12 the last event (the key of what to say).
