librarygames/horse-race/HorseRace.xtl

The horse race's rules, a library: horse-race.xtl (a scripted race), play.xtl (at the terminal) and the web page all use these. Five horses, a track of 15; every round each horse runs 1, 2 or 3 at random, all at once: the field is one vector of positions and a round is one expression. The first round in which any horse reaches the finish ends the race; the horses furthest along win (there can be a tie).
