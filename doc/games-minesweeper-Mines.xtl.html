librarygames/minesweeper/Mines.xtl

Minesweeper's rules, a library: minesweeper.xtl (scripted), play.xtl (at the terminal) and the web page all use these. A 9 by 9 field hides 10 mines; open a square to see how many mines touch it, flag the ones you think are mines, and open every safe square to win.

The state is one vector: the status (0 playing, 1 won, 2 lost), then the mines, the opened squares and the flags, three 9 by 9 0/1 boards.
