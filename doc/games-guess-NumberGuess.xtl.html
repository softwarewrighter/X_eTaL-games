librarygames/guess/NumberGuess.xtl

Guess the number's rules, a library: guess.xtl (scripted games), play.xtl (at the terminal) and the web page all use these. The computer thinks of a number from 1 to 100; each guess is answered higher, lower or correct.

Narrow every range by the answers to its guess: a higher answer raises the low end past the guess, a lower one drops the high end below it (a max and a min that leave the other answers alone).
