programgames/shut-the-box/play.xtl

Shut the box at the terminal: roll, then type the tiles to shut (they must add up to the roll, for example "2 5"). The moves you have are listed each turn. The game ends when no open tiles make the roll; the open tiles are your score, and shutting them all wins. The rules are those of ShutTheBox.xtl.
