programgames/dungeon/play.xtl

The dungeon: find the way out (>). Arrow keys or WASD move you; walk into a monster to fight it; walk over potions (!) and gold ($) to pick them up; r starts again. Each game is a new dungeon (a map and places drawn from its seed); a game won or lost ends the program (on the page, Play or any key then starts a new one). On the page the dungeon is in a dialog and your keys come to the program as events; at the command line, lines such as "key Up" on standard input. The rules are Crawl.xtl's, built on lib/Ecs.
