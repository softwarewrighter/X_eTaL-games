librarygames/blocks/Stack.xtl

The falling blocks' rules, a library: blocks.xtl (scripted), play.xtl (the game: keys and the clock) and the web page all use these. The well is a matrix (0 empty, else the color of the piece that left a block there) and a piece is a small square matrix of 0s and 1s: it turns by reversing its rows and transposing, it fits where every one of its blocks lands on an empty cell, full rows are found by a row-wise and, and cleared by keeping only the other rows. A game's state is a tuple: (the well, the piece's shape, its kind, its row and column, the next kind, the score, the lines, the seconds toward the next step down, the seconds played, how it stands: 0 playing, 2 the well full).
