programgames/horse-race/play.xtl

Horse race at the terminal: you start with 10 coins. Before each race pick a horse, 1 to 5, and bet a coin on it; if it wins (a share of a tie counts) you get 4 back. Type q to stop. The rules are those of HorseRace.xtl.
