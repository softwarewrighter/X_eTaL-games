librarygames/raycaster/Rays.xtl

The raycaster's rules, a library: raycaster.xtl (scripted), play.xtl (the game: keys and the clock) and the web page all use these. The map is a grid of walls; you stand anywhere in it, facing any way. The view is one ray per column of the picture, every ray stepped through the grid at once (each to its next line across or down, whichever is nearer) until all of them have hit a wall: a fixed point. A wall's column is as tall as it is near. A game's state is a tuple: (where you are, x and y in tiles, y down; which way you face, radians, 0 east, turning right as it grows; the seconds played; how it stands: 0 walking, 1 out of the door).
