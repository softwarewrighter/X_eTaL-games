librarygames/robot-chase/RobotChase.xtl

Robot chase's rules, a library: robot-chase.xtl (scripted), play.xtl (at the terminal) and the web page all use these. A 16 by 16 board, you and 12 robots. Every turn you step (or wait, or teleport), then every robot steps one square toward you, all at once. Robots that land on the same square crash and leave a wreck; a robot that walks into a wreck is destroyed. Clear the board to win; meet a robot or a wreck and you lose. Ported from the COR24 BASIC robot-chase.bas.

The game is one flat vector of numbers (so a page can hand it back in): player row and column (0 to 15), teleports left, turn, the last event, the status (0 playing, 1 won, 2 lost), then the robots' rows, their columns, which are alive (12 each), and the wrecks (256 cells).
