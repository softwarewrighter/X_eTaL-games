programgames/trek/play.xtl

Star Trek at the terminal, as the COR24 BASIC game plays it: destroy every Klingon before the stardate runs out. Commands by number (9 is help); warp, impulse, phasers, torpedoes and shields then ask for a course (1 north, clockwise to 8) and a number. The rules are those of StarTrek.xtl.
