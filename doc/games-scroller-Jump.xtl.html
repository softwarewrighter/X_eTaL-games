librarygames/scroller/Jump.xtl

The side-scroller's rules, a library: scroller.xtl (scripted), play.xtl (the game: keys and the clock) and the web page all use these. Built on lib/Ecs: a world is a matrix, a row per entity, and every rule is a system, one expression over every entity with the components it needs. You, the walkers, the coins and the flag are entities; gravity, movement and collision with the ground act on every body at once, you and the walkers alike. A game's state is a tuple: (the world, the coins picked up, the seconds played, how it stands: 0 playing, 1 you reached the flag, 2 you were caught or fell).
