librarygames/lights-out/Lamps.xtl

Lights out's rules, a solver and the board's picture, a library: lights-out.xtl (scripted), play.xtl (the game: a board you click) and the web page all use these. The board is 25 lights, row by row, 1 on. A press toggles a light and its four neighbors: the press matrix, 25 by 25, says which, and a whole board of presses is one product with it, mod 2. A game's state: the moves made, the square a hint shows (0 none), then the 25 lights.

Solving it

ʰs̲tep : Int -> (Int, Int) -> Int

function (private) · line 41

One column of Gaussian elimination mod 2 on the augmented matrix m (the press matrix, the board as its last column), (r, c) the next pivot row and the column: a row with a 1 in the column is swapped up, and added (mod 2) to every other row with a 1 there; a column without one is a free press, left 0.

ʰs̲tep ← { m (r, c) →
  (c > 25) ∨ r > 25 ? m
  cand ← w̲here (1 = c s̲elect₂ m) ∧ (r̲ange 25) ≥ r
  0 = t̲ally cand ? m ʰs̲tep (r, c + 1)
  p ← f̲irst cand
  k ← r̲ange 25
  m ← (k + ((p − r) × k = r) + (r − p) × k = p) s̲elect m
  hit ← 0 + (1 = c s̲elect₂ m) ∧ k ≠ r
  m ← (m + hit '× t̲able r s̲elect m) m̲od 2
  m ʰs̲tep (r + 1, c + 1)
}
