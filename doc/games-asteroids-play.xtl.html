programgames/asteroids/play.xtl

Asteroids: break every rock, three waves of them. Left and right (or a and d) turn the ship, up (or w) thrusts, the space bar (or f) fires; a big rock breaks into two medium ones, a medium into two small; a rock that hits the ship costs a life; r starts again. The game moves with the clock: on the page it is in a dialog, and the clock's ticks (twenty a second) and your keys come to the program as events; at the command line, lines such as "tick 0.05", "key LEFT" and "key space" on standard input. When every wave is cleared or the lives are gone the program ends with the score (on the page, Play or any key then starts a new game). The rules are Rocks.xtl's, built on lib/Ecs.
