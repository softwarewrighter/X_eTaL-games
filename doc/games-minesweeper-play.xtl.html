programgames/minesweeper/play.xtl

Minesweeper at the terminal: a 9 by 9 field with 10 mines. Type a row and a column to open a square ("5 5"), or f and a row and a column to flag one ("f 4 4"). Open every safe square to win. The rules are those of Mines.xtl.
