programgames/2048/play.xtl

2048 at the terminal: slide the tiles with a (left), d (right), w (up) or s (down), then Enter; q stops. Equal tiles that meet merge; make a 2048. The board is printed as X_eTaL prints a matrix (0 is an empty square). The rules are those of Twenty48.xtl.
