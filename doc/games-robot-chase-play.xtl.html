programgames/robot-chase/play.xtl

Robot chase at the terminal, as the COR24 BASIC game plays it: move with the numeric keypad (7 8 9 up, 4 6 sideways, 5 waits, 1 2 3 down), 0 teleports (3 a game), 10 scans the board in 4 by 4 regions, 99 resigns. Make the robots crash into each other or into wrecks; clear them all to win. The rules are those of RobotChase.xtl.
