programgames/tower-defense/play.xtl

Tower defense: stop the creeps on the road before they get out. Pick a tower on the toolbar (or 1, 2, 3; left and right step through them), click a grass tile to build it there, click a tower to upgrade it; Next wave (or the space bar) sends the next wave; the Grimoire (or g) shows every tower and creep, turning, with what it does. Six waves to beat; a creep that gets out costs lives. The game moves with the clock: on the page it is in a dialog, and the clock's ticks (twenty a second), your clicks and your keys come to the program as events; at the command line, lines such as "tick 0.05", "click 384 192" and "key space" on standard input. When the waves are beaten or the lives are gone the program ends (on the page, Play or any key then starts a new game). The rules are Defense.xtl's, built on lib/Ecs.
