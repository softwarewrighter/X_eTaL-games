programgames/sudoku/play.xtl

Sudoku at the terminal. Choose a puzzle (1 easiest, 4 hardest) or type or paste your own (81 cells, row by row, 0 or . empty), then type a row, a column and a digit ("1 3 4"; digit 0 clears the cell), h for a hint, c and a row and a column for a cell's candidates ("c 1 3"), s to see the solution, q to stop. The rules are those of SudokuGrid.xtl.
