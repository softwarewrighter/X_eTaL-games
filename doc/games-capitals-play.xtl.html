programgames/capitals/play.xtl

Capitals: a map you click. Each round puts five red dots on the map, each a national capital (or a place of your own) inside its unlabeled country; click a dot, then its name among four nearby cities. The buttons along the top start a round in a region or the whole world. On the page the map is in a dialog and your clicks come to the program as events; at the command line, lines such as "click 1234 567" (map units) on standard input. The rules and the drawing are Atlas.xtl's.
