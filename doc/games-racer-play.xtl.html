programgames/racer/play.xtl

The racer: three laps against three rivals. Up (or w) speeds you up, down (or s) slows you, left and right (or a and d) steer; keep off the grass, which slows you; r starts again. The race moves with the clock: on the page it is in a dialog, and the clock's ticks (twenty a second) and your keys come to the program as events; at the command line, lines such as "tick 0.05" and "key UP" on standard input. When you finish the program ends with your place (on the page, Play or any key then starts a new race). The rules are Race.xtl's, built on lib/Ecs.
